FireRisk Open
v0.1 · research prototype

Open satellite fire-detection forecasting.

A reproducible dataset and experimental model for predicting whether a geographic cell will have a newly observed satellite fire detection on the following UTC day.

Not for emergency decisions. This predicts satellite detections, not actual ignition probabilities, fire spread, evacuation needs, or guaranteed absence of fire. Predictions are unavailable until trained and validated with real observations.
