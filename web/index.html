FireRisk Open
v0.3 · research prototype · 6 regions

Next-day satellite fire-detection probabilities.

For each 0.5° land cell with no satellite fire detection in the past 7 days: the calibrated probability that NASA's NOAA-20 VIIRS sensor records a new qualifying fire detection there during the next UTC day (24–48 h after the 00:00 UTC issue time). Built from an open, reproducible dataset; evaluated once on regions and years never used for development.

Not for emergency decisions. Not for evacuation, suppression, dispatch or public alerts. This is a satellite-detection probability, not a probability of ignition, spread or danger; clouds, smoke and small fires cause missed detections, and agricultural/prescribed burning counts as detections. Live inputs (near-real-time FIRMS, GEOS-IT weather) differ from the training inputs and are not separately validated.

Limitations — read before using

  • Experimental research output. Not an official forecast, fire-danger rating, alert or warning.
  • Satellite detection, not wildfire risk. The probability is that NOAA-20 VIIRS records a qualifying thermal anomaly in a ~2,300 km² cell during the next UTC day. It says nothing about ignition, fire behaviour, spread, smoke, or danger to people or property.
  • Misses and false fires. Clouds, smoke, small or short fires and gaps between overpasses cause missed detections; agricultural, prescribed and some industrial heat sources are counted.
  • Modest skill. On held-out 2024–25 data the model ranks cells better than a fire climatology, but in never-used regions only about 1 in 7 alerts was followed by a detection, and in the Ozarks the gain over climatology was not statistically clear.
  • Live accuracy is unknown. Live inputs (near-real-time FIRMS, GEOS-IT weather) differ from the archived training inputs; accuracy on live predictions is reported only after enough real outcomes have been logged.
  • Six regions only. Nothing is predicted or implied outside the boxes shown; cells with a detection in the last 7 days are not scored.
