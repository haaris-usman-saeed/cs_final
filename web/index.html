FireRisk Open
v0.2 · research prototype

Open satellite fire-detection forecasting.

An original, reproducible dataset and experimental model estimating whether a 0.5° grid cell in Northern California will have a new NOAA-20 VIIRS active-fire detection during the next UTC day (24–48 h after the 00:00 UTC issue time).

Not for emergency decisions. Not for evacuation, suppression, dispatch or public-safety alerts. This estimates a satellite detection, not ignition, spread or danger. A non-detection can mean cloud, smoke, or a fire too small to see. Live inputs (NRT fires, forecast-model weather) differ from the training inputs (standard-processing fires, ERA5) and are unvalidated.
